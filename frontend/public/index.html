Our Story

BORN AT KNUST

Burger Yard started with one idea: KNUST students deserve better than average campus food. We set up right here, near Lienda Ville Hostel, to serve the hardest-working students in Ghana with burgers that actually hit.

Every patty is fresh, every bun is toasted, every sauce is made in-house. We're your campus neighbour, and we take pride in every single order.
